## Prompt

Create a sophisticated, mesmerizing abstract technology background for a
premium open-source coding-agent plugin catalog. Use a deep midnight-navy
spatial field with subtle layered depth, elegant flowing luminous filaments,
and small connected light nodes suggesting knowledge, tools, and agent
orchestration. Keep cyan, electric blue, and violet dominant, with only a very
small amount of warm coral light near the outer edges.

Use a wide 16:10 composition. Preserve generous quiet negative space in the
upper-left for a title and throughout the central field for three rows of
cards. Concentrate detail around the perimeter and corners, with graceful flow
that guides the eye inward. Avoid an obvious horizon or floor grid. The result
should feel crisp, refined, intelligent, confident, and understated, with
subtle volumetric glow and fine detail that survives downscaling.

Background only: no text, letters, logos, icons, cards, UI elements, or
watermarks. Avoid a neon tunnel, racing light trails, cheesy cyberpunk,
rainbow overload, a busy center, hard lens flares, low-resolution gradients,
banding, or visual noise.
